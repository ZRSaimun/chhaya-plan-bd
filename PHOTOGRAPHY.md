# Photographic experience

Replaces the low-poly introduction with licensed photography, slow camera movement, pointer parallax and themed ambient light. No video, generated scenery or third-party animation library. Pauses when hidden or outside the viewport. Reduced motion starts with movement off; a manual toggle is provided. Pointer movement is decorative and does not intercept touch scrolling.
