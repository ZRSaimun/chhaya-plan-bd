# Next increments

1. **Dhaka pilot dataset:** one reviewed ward, reproducible canopy calculation, date/accuracy notes and no overlapping boundaries. Show unknown instead of invented values.
2. **Map:** actual ward/site geometries, source/version labels, canopy and heat overlays. No decorative pins suggesting surveyed sites.
3. **Reviewed species library:** local expert-screened native candidates by site conditions, not an automatic prescriptive species ranking. Quantity allocation only after planting-site assessment.
4. **Priority comparison:** transparent low-canopy/heat/exposure/access indicators; missing-data handling and editable weights, no claim of national representativeness.
5. **Sensitivity ranges:** show how crown size, survival and available land alter estimates; distinguish statistical uncertainty from user-selected scenarios.
6. **Maintenance budget:** seedlings, site preparation, guards, watering, pruning and replacements using dated local supplier evidence. Separate capital and recurring costs.
7. **Cohort tracking:** plant date, species, public site ID, repeated 3/6/12/24-month inspections, survival and replacement records; prevent double counting.
8. **Data collaboration:** reviewed public-site submissions, consent and moderation; no private locations or unverified public planting claims.
9. **Bangla localisation:** full reviewed Bangla interface, screen-reader checks and phone-width testing.
10. **National expansion:** extend beyond Dhaka only when local boundaries, ecology and maintainers are available; never extrapolate Dhaka assumptions to the whole country.

No chatbot is required for the first useful release. If added later, it must retrieve cited reviewed records and state unknowns, not invent tree quotas or ecological guidance.
