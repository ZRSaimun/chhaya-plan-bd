ILLUSTRATIVE INPUTS

How the estimate works

Canopy gap = area × positive canopy-percentage gap. Effective crown = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × (1 − overlap discount). Survivor target = canopy gap ÷ effective crown, rounded up. Initial planting target adjusts for survival. Space capacity = available land ÷ spacing², rounded down.

This simple model omits growth time, shade direction, underground services, competing vegetation and detailed crown geometry. The survival rate must match the planning horizon. Newly planted trees do not deliver mature canopy immediately.

02 / RIGHT TREE, RIGHT PLACE

Species decisions need a site.

Do not apply a Dhaka street palette to a wetland, coast or natural grassland.

Dhaka research candidates · not site-approved

A 2021 Dhaka road-divider study names the following among candidates for wider dividers with deeper soil. These are research leads, not a blanket street recommendation. A local specialist must assess each site; none has a quantity allocated here.

  • Palash — Butea monosperma
  • Shimul — Bombax ceiba
  • Sal — Shorea robusta
  • Chatim — Alstonia scholaris
  • Kadam — Neolamarckia cadamba
  • Jam — Syzygium cumini

01

Survey the space

Measure rootable soil, overhead cables, buildings, paths, drains and future crown clearance.

02

Review the ecology

Check native habitat, salinity, flooding, soil, water demand, invasive risk and nursery provenance with a local specialist.

03

Diversify & maintain

Agree an appropriate species mix, funded watering, replacement policy and monitoring before ordering saplings.

04 / WHAT WE KNOW, WHAT WE NEED

Evidence before an exact number.

Canopy measurement

Use dated imagery, a documented classification method, boundary version and accuracy review. A 10 m land-cover pixel is not an individual tree count.

Site feasibility

Ground-check ownership, utilities, soil, water, accessibility and environmental constraints. Unbuilt land is not automatically plantable land.

Priority, not certainty

Combine low canopy with measured heat and exposure around schools, clinics and pedestrian routes. Publish data gaps and uncertainty.
